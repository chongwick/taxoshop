This PR introduces a use of uninitialized memory.

- A new `PyFunctionObject` is allocated [here](https://github.com/python/cpython/blob/64f99350351bc46e016b2286f36ba7cd669b79e3/Objects/funcobject.c#L114)

- Which reads from `->func_version` [here](https://github.com/python/cpython/blob/64f99350351bc46e016b2286f36ba7cd669b79e3/Objects/funcobject.c#L272)
- However, `->func_version` was never initialized
